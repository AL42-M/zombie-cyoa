    - "Give Bram the count."
    - "Go ask Teddy what he wants."
    - "Ask her whether anybody has gotten out of this town."
    - "Back out. Cut through the Fenwick Houses instead."
    - "Why do you stay?"

    - **Endings** acknowledge exactly who is with you, and what you know.
- **Help text** that explains the four statuses plainly.

### Changed
- **American English everywhere**, set in the US, keeping the distinctive names. Examples:
    - lorry → truck
    - wind-up torch → crank flashlight
    - hoover → vacuum cleaner
    - surgical spirit → rubbing alcohol
    - "Ring Mum" → "Call Mom"
    - "Eleven years on prison landings" → "Eleven years on cell blocks"
    - "A woman in a tower block told me" → "A woman in a high-rise told me"
    - St. Amaranth Mercy, Dr. Aytoun, month/day dates
- **AI phrasing removed** across the whole game. Examples:
    - "this isn't an infection. It's a recruitment." → "it is *recruiting* me. It wants me out in the
    street with the others, holding the note."
    - "Not words. Not quite voices." → "a crowd holding one low note, too even to be voices."
    - "The humming is not a sound; it is *load-bearing*." → "The humming holds it together."
    - "That is all it takes. It isn't surrender, and it isn't a fall. It feels like…" → "That is all it
    takes. It feels like putting down a bag I have carried so long that my arm stopped noticing it."
    - "That's not a threat, it's a courtesy." → "She means it as a courtesy…"
    - "doing something that isn't calm but is wearing calm's clothes" → "She keeps her face still the
    whole time, and I can see in her jaw what that costs her."

    - "a kindness shaped like abandonment" → "he stays behind alone, and the rest of you leave quietly"
    - "the hard road" → "slow and loud, and everyone is exposed"
    - "the only steady hands left" → "she'll tell you how long he has"
    - "Heavy to carry. Heavier to leave." → "You take the bolt cutters. They're heavy."
    - "the last handwriting of the old world" → "somebody tried to warn whoever came next"
- **Voices sharpened:**
    - Odile never uses contractions.
    - Bram stays plain and procedural.
    - Teddy speaks in short breaths and rarely says "child".
    - The Matron is formal, Dr. Aytoun is tired and plain, and the hospital staff sound like ordinary
    Americans.
